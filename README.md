A one-page newspaper front page summing up Thursday 26 November 2026 in 36 dated stories across 9 regions: the Macy's 100th Thanksgiving parade postponed by a nor'easter, India marking 18 years since the 26/11 Mumbai attacks, and Gukesh meeting Sindarov in Game 3 of the world chess title match — with a UTC timeline, a world map with numbered pins, a numbered list of the remaining stories, and a date check that leaves out earlier news on purpose. Every word is rendered from code, so the page is sharp at any size; the three lead scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
